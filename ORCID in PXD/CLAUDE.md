# ORCID in PXD — Claude Bootstrap
## Identity
- **Project:** ORCID in PXD
- **References:** DTP1092 (current) | ITS1214 (origin)
- **Purpose:** Adding ORCID capture and management into PeopleXD, covering onboarding (prompting new starters to register their ORCID via IT Registration) and self-service (staff can enter/amend their ORCID at any time). Surfaces ORCID as a person identifier alongside SSO, pushing value into the Data Warehouse for research and reporting (REF compliance).
